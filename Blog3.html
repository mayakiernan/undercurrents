Notes & sources

Figures are rounded and written for a general reader. Zones on the map are illustrative, not hazard models. Catastrophe-loss estimates differ by firm and are revised over time.

  • Swiss Re Institute, sigma natural-catastrophe reports (2025 insured losses of about $107 billion; 92% from wildfire, severe convective storm and flood): via Artemis
  • Insurance Information Institute: severe convective storms above $50 billion in insured losses for a third straight year; hail up to 80% of those claims: via InsuranceNewsNet
  • Cotality (formerly CoreLogic), 2026: hail now a leading driver of insured losses, on par with major hurricanes: press release
  • Allianz Commercial: hail drives as much as 80% of severe-convective-storm insured losses: via Business Insurance
  • kWh Analytics, Solar Risk Assessment: hail is 73% of solar losses by dollar value but about 6% of loss events: via pv magazine
